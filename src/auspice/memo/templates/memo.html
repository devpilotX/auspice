how this number was produced, and what it does not know

No language model produced this probability. Language models read the source documents and nothing else. The number comes from a statistical model fitted to historical decisions, which is what makes it possible to back test, calibrate, and publish an accuracy record against. Every quote in this memo was checked character for character against the document it came from; quotes that did not match were discarded rather than flagged.
