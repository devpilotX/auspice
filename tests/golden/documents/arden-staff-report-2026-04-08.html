Analysis

The application proposes a data centre of up to 180 MW on a parcel already zoned M-1. The use is permitted in M-1 only by special use permit, so the Board may lawfully refuse it even where every dimensional standard is met.

The applicant satisfies the height, lot coverage and parking standards of the district. Staff notes that the parcel lies 1,450 feet from the nearest residentially zoned property, which is 550 feet short of the 2,000 foot setback established by the Data Centre Overlay District, adopted 14 September 2025. The applicant has not requested a variance from that standard.

Staff further notes that the county has denied three of the last four applications of this type, and that the Board adopted the overlay district in direct response to applications of this kind.

For the reasons above, staff recommends denial.

Conditions, if the Board is inclined to approve

  1. Relocation of the buildable area to achieve the 2,000 foot setback.
  2. A groundwater impact study prepared by a licensed hydrogeologist, filed before site plan approval.
  3. A noise study demonstrating compliance with 55 dBA at the property line.
