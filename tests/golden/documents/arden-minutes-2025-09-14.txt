BOARD OF SUPERVISORS
COUNTY OF ARDEN, VIRGINIA
REGULAR MEETING
Minutes of 14 September 2025

PRESENT: Chair M. Okafor, Supervisor J. Alvarez, Supervisor R. Boone,
Supervisor T. Whitfield, Supervisor D. Ramirez, Supervisor L. Chen
ABSENT: Supervisor K. Nakamura

The Chair called the meeting to order at 6:03 p.m. and confirmed a quorum.

---

ITEM 4. REZ-2025-0114 and SUP-2025-0061.
Application of Ridgeline Digital Holdings LLC for a rezoning of approximately
412 acres from A-1 Agricultural to PD-I Planned Development Industrial, and for a
special use permit to permit a data centre campus of up to 300 megawatts.
Property: tax parcels 0912-44-0031 and 0912-44-0032, Pageland Road.

Staff presentation by the Director of Planning. Staff recommended approval with
conditions, noting that the application satisfies the dimensional standards of the
PD-I district and that the applicant has offered a 400 foot vegetated buffer along
the northern property line.

The Planning Commission recommended denial by a vote of 5 to 2 on 20 August 2025.

Public comment opened at 6:41 p.m. Forty-one speakers were heard, of whom
thirty-three spoke in opposition. The Arden Groundwater Alliance, represented by
counsel, submitted a petition bearing 1,240 signatures. Speakers raised groundwater
withdrawal, noise from mechanical cooling, and the effect of the facility on
residential property values to the north.

Supervisor Alvarez stated that she could not support the application until the
county understood what it would do to the aquifer, and noted that she had been
asking the same question for six months.

Supervisor Boone spoke in favour, citing the tax base and the applicant's offer of
a community benefit agreement.

MOTION: Supervisor Boone moved to approve REZ-2025-0114 and SUP-2025-0061 subject
to the conditions in the staff report. The motion failed on a vote of 2 to 4, with
Supervisor Nakamura absent.

Voting in favour: Boone, Chen.
Voting against: Okafor, Alvarez, Whitfield, Ramirez.

The applications are therefore denied.

---

ITEM 5. ZOA-2025-0007.
A county initiated amendment to Chapter 18 of the Zoning Ordinance to establish a
Data Centre Overlay District, imposing a minimum setback of 2,000 feet from any
residentially zoned parcel, a noise limit of 55 dBA measured at the property line,
and a cap on potable water withdrawal of 250,000 gallons per day.

MOTION: Supervisor Whitfield moved to adopt the amendment as presented. The motion
carried on a vote of 5 to 1, with Supervisor Nakamura absent. The amendment is
effective upon adoption.

Voting in favour: Okafor, Alvarez, Whitfield, Ramirez, Chen.
Voting against: Boone.

---

ITEM 6. REZ-2025-0098.
Application of Talbot Warehouse Partners LP for a rezoning of 61 acres from A-1 to
M-1 Light Industrial to permit a distribution centre.

Staff recommended approval. No speakers appeared in opposition.

MOTION: Supervisor Ramirez moved to approve REZ-2025-0098. The motion carried
unanimously.

---

ITEM 7. REZ-2024-0210.
Application of Halloway Solar I LLC, continued from the meeting of 11 May 2025 at
the applicant's request. The applicant has requested a further continuance to the
meeting of 9 November 2025.

MOTION: Supervisor Chen moved to continue the application to 9 November 2025. The
motion carried on a vote of 6 to 0.
